About SkillBridge

SkillBridge is a student-focused platform designed to help students understand their skills and improve them step by step.

What SkillBridge Provides

  • Skill Assessment
  • Personalized Learning Roadmap
  • Progress Tracking
  • Internship Preparation

Our Goal

Our goal is to help students identify skill gaps, follow the right learning path, build projects, and become internship ready.
